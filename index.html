Why I Built This Project

This independent project brings infrastructure locations and public-sector reporting into one interface, combining police, fire-station and hospital maps with historical crime, emergency-service and healthcare reports.

  • Explore infrastructure locations geographically.
  • Bring multi-sector indicators into one executive overview.
  • Present historical trends with data definitions and limitations.
  • Demonstrate the intersection of security operations, analysis and technology.

Scope: historical analytical dashboard, not a live monitoring service or official New Zealand government platform.

How this portal works: each map or report opens inside this page. The original map dashboards and the three analytical reports are embedded in this single file, so you do not need to keep their source HTML files alongside the portal. Internet access is still needed for their online map tiles and CDN libraries. Source counts are reproduced from the supplied dashboards and may use different counting definitions.
